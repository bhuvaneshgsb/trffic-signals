Important Road Safety Tips

Wear Helmet

Always wear a helmet while riding a two-wheeler to protect your head from serious injuries.

Use Seat Belt

Seat belts reduce the risk of injury during accidents and are mandatory for all passengers.

Follow Speed Limits

Driving within speed limits prevents accidents and ensures safety for everyone on the road.

Avoid Mobile Usage

Using a mobile phone while driving can cause distractions and serious accidents.

Don’t Drink and Drive

Drunk driving is dangerous and punishable by law. Always drive responsibly.

Respect Pedestrians

Always give way to pedestrians at crossings and zebra lines.
