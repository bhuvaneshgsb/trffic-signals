Traffic rules are designed to keep everyone safe on the road. This website helps you understand road signs, driving rules, and safety tips in a simple and clear way.

Important Traffic Rules

Stop Rule

Always stop your vehicle at a red light or stop sign.

Speed Limit

Do not exceed the speed limit to avoid accidents.

Seat Belt

Wearing a seat belt is mandatory for safety.

No Honking

Avoid unnecessary honking, especially in silence zones.
