Important Traffic Signs

Stop Sign

Come to a complete stop before proceeding.

No Entry

Vehicles are not allowed to enter this road.

Speed Limit

Maximum speed allowed on this road.

No Parking

Parking is not allowed in this area.

School Zone

Slow down. Children may be crossing.

Pedestrian Crossing

Give way to pedestrians crossing the road.
